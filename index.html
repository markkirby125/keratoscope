Keratoscope

A presentation tweak that may reduce perceived text doubling for some users — no guarantee.

Your system is set to dark mode. Keratoscope currently works best in light mode; a dark theme is planned.

Safety check

New or sudden double vision can be a sign of a serious eye or neurological problem. If your double vision is new or sudden, see an eye professional today.

Keratoscope is a comfort aid, not a medical device, and not a substitute for professional eye care.
